ADDING OR REPLACING MATERIAL
Use materials.html to enter links and export a catalog, or review and edit
assets/catalog.json. The form does not write that file automatically.
Use relative paths for assets shared with a clone; commit the assets and catalog.
Browser-only links do not travel with Git. External URLs may need a network.
Images can appear in the viewer; GIS, models and data files are collected for
later integration and are not parsed by the viewer.

Keep source type, creator, permissions, dimensions, caption and applicability.
For synthetic material use a clear illustrative label and no invented capture
time. For actual material use its real acquisition time and update the replay
range accordingly. Keep captions in English and preserve original tool results.

Both plots use raw zero-based ridge IDs 0–63; target 20–39 means 20 ridges.
soybean_field = farm plot A; maize_field = farm plot B.
Do not display event support before its applicable step or reuse a static
reference as a live observation. No photographs, spectral/thermal rasters,
bounding boxes or segmentation masks are present in the recorded tool output.
Disease pressure is a model index, not a measured diseased-leaf percentage.
Events 110 and 115 have no valid NDVI and use no NDVI example image.
