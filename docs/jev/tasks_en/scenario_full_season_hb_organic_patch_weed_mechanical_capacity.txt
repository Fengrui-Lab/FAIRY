Task: manage early patchy weed pressure in a Heinong 84 standard-density soybean field under organic constraints. Conventional herbicides are not allowed; mechanical weeding capacity is enough for only 16 ridges. Use the whole-field state, NDVI, the drone and robot/ground checks to separate high/medium/low weed patches, and mechanically weed only the patches with the highest pressure that most need protection.
Known field and planting plan: 64 ridges, wide-ridge dense planting with two rows per ridge, ridge spacing 1.1 m, standard sowing depth 4.0 cm. ridges 0–63: HEINONG84, standard density, seed_spacing_cm=7.9 (plant spacing 7.9 cm), about 23.0 plants/m² (about 230,000 plants/ha).
Management focus: under the organic constraint and limited mechanical capacity, follow the loop "whole-field state finds the anomaly → drone locates high/medium/low patches → robot or ground check confirms weeds and trafficability → treat only the highest-pressure patches → recheck recovery", and avoid mistaking green weed cover for crop health.
Follow these steps:
1) Plant ridges 0-63: first confirm the seedbed, soil moisture, weather window, equipment and seed inventory; the purpose is to establish this scenario's variety, density and whole-field crop stand.
2) Mechanical weed control on the highest-pressure patches: first use the whole-field state and NDVI to find suspected weed pressure, then use the drone to locate the weed differences between ridges 0-15, 16-31 and 32-63, and finally use the robot or a ground check to review weed cover, inter-row competition and soil trafficability; under the organic constraint, mechanically weed only the 16-ridge patch with the highest pressure where the soil allows field entry.
3) Harvest, then unload grain / dry if needed / store, for confirmed mature areas: confirm the area's maturity, grain moisture, weather and field trafficability; the purpose is to recover that area's yield in its window and store it safely.
Overall constraints: before every key treatment, first confirm the corresponding field evidence and resource status; chemical herbicides must not be used; mechanical weeding covers at most 16 ridges, the whole field cannot be weeded mechanically. Harvest only when the crop is mature, grain moisture is known, the weather allows harvesting and the field is trafficable; batches at ≤13.5% moisture can be stored directly, wetter batches must first be dried to a safe moisture.
