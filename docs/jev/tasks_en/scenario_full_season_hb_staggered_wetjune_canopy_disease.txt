Task: manage the wet-June disease risk in a Heinong 84 field planted at staggered dates. Check canopy closure, NDVI, thermal signals and ground disease zone by zone, and treat only confirmed areas.
Known field and planting plan: 64 ridges, wide-ridge dense planting with two rows per ridge, ridge spacing 1.1 m, standard sowing depth 4.0 cm. ridges 0–31: HEINONG84, standard density, seed_spacing_cm=7.9 (plant spacing 7.9 cm), about 23.0 plants/m² (about 230,000 plants/ha), early-planted zone; ridges 32–63: HEINONG84, standard density, seed_spacing_cm=7.9 (plant spacing 7.9 cm), about 23.0 plants/m² (about 230,000 plants/ha), late-planted zone.
Management focus: schedule harvest, drying and storage batches according to R8, grain_moisture, the weather window and drying/storage capacity.
Follow these steps:
1) Plant ridges 0-31: first confirm the seedbed, soil moisture, weather window, equipment and seed inventory; the purpose is to establish this scenario's variety, density and zoning.
2) Plant ridges 32-63: first confirm the seedbed, soil moisture, weather window, equipment and seed inventory; the purpose is to establish this scenario's variety, density and zoning.
3) Fungicide for confirmed areas: first use sensors/canopy state to find suspected anomalies, then use the drone to locate canopy or NDVI anomaly areas, and finally use the robot or a ground check to confirm lesions, disease confirmation signals and spraying weather; only when there is enough evidence to treat the disease, apply fungicide to the confirmed disease areas.
4) Harvest by zone, then unload grain / dry if needed / store, for the corresponding mature zone: confirm the zone's maturity, grain moisture, weather and field trafficability; the purpose is to recover that zone's yield in its window and store it safely.
Overall constraints: before every key treatment, first confirm the corresponding field evidence and resource status; do not treat the whole field uniformly just because a risk exists, and do not use one treatment to solve a different kind of stress. Harvest only when the crop is mature, grain moisture is known, the weather allows harvesting and the field is trafficable; batches at ≤13.5% moisture can be stored directly, wetter batches must first be dried to a safe moisture.
