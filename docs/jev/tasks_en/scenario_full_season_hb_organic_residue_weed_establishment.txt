Task: manage a high-residue, wet-cold spring soybean field under organic / permitted-input constraints. Check the seedbed, emergence and weeds, and use the permitted mechanical/monitoring paths; conventional chemical herbicides must not be used.
Known field and planting plan: 64 ridges, wide-ridge dense planting with two rows per ridge, ridge spacing 1.1 m, standard sowing depth 4.0 cm. ridges 0–63: HEINONG84, standard density, seed_spacing_cm=7.9 (plant spacing 7.9 cm), about 23.0 plants/m² (about 230,000 plants/ha).
Management focus: decide whether to control weeds based on weed cover, inter-row competition and crop growth, and avoid mistaking green weed cover for crop health.
Follow these steps:
1) Base fertilizer, whole field: complete the basic nutrient input according to the pre-planting fertility plan and fertilizer inventory; the purpose is to avoid a basic nutrient shortage interfering with later diagnosis.
2) Plant ridges 0-63: first confirm the seedbed, soil moisture, weather window, equipment and seed inventory; the purpose is to establish this scenario's variety, density and zoning.
3) Weed control in confirmed areas: first use the whole-field state and NDVI to find abnormal greenness or slow canopy closure, then use the drone to distinguish weed greenness from crop growth in the field NDVI, and finally use the robot or a ground check to review weed cover and inter-row competition; control weeds only in areas with confirmed weed competition.
4) Replant confirmed gap areas: based on emergence rate, stand gaps and the remaining season length, replant only the areas where it still pays off; the purpose is to restore the stand in gap areas and reduce early stand loss.
5) Harvest, then unload grain / dry if needed / store, for confirmed mature areas: confirm the area's maturity, grain moisture, weather and field trafficability; the purpose is to recover that area's yield in its window and store it safely.
Overall constraints: before every key treatment, first confirm the corresponding field evidence and resource status; do not treat the whole field uniformly just because a risk exists, and do not use one treatment to solve a different kind of stress. Harvest only when the crop is mature, grain moisture is known, the weather allows harvesting and the field is trafficable; batches at ≤13.5% moisture can be stored directly, wetter batches must first be dried to a safe moisture.
