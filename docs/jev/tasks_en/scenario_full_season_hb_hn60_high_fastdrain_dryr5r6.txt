Task: manage the local R5/R6 water-shortage risk in a high-density Heinong 60 field. Use soil, thermal signals and canopy state to locate the truly water-short areas, and irrigate only those areas.
Known field and planting plan: 64 ridges, wide-ridge dense planting with two rows per ridge, ridge spacing 1.1 m, standard sowing depth 4.0 cm. ridges 0–63: HEINONG60, high density, seed_spacing_cm=6.8 (plant spacing 6.8 cm), about 26.7 plants/m² (about 267,000 plants/ha); target density about 29 plants/m².
Management focus: when water amount or the moisture window is limited, decide whether to irrigate specific areas based on soil moisture/VWC, canopy thermal anomalies, growth stage and variety stress tolerance.
Follow these steps:
1) Plant ridges 0-63: first confirm the seedbed, soil moisture, weather window, equipment and seed inventory; the purpose is to establish this scenario's variety, density and zoning.
2) Irrigate confirmed areas: first use soil moisture and crop state to find falling soil moisture/VWC or canopy thermal anomalies, then use the drone/thermal signal to locate the water-risk area, and finally use the robot or a ground check to confirm it is not a pest, disease, weed or nutrition problem; taking growth stage and the water budget into account, irrigate only the areas confirmed to be short of water and worth the water.
3) Harvest, then unload grain / dry if needed / store, for confirmed mature areas: confirm the area's maturity, grain moisture, weather and field trafficability; the purpose is to recover that area's yield in its window and store it safely.
Overall constraints: before every key treatment, first confirm the corresponding field evidence and resource status; do not treat the whole field uniformly just because a risk exists, and do not use one treatment to solve a different kind of stress. Harvest only when the crop is mature, grain moisture is known, the weather allows harvesting and the field is trafficable; batches at ≤13.5% moisture can be stored directly, wetter batches must first be dried to a safe moisture.
